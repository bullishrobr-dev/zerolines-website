Your Photograph

What becomes of the picture.

  • It is read by a machine, not a person. The assessment is written by a large language model — currently OpenAI's GPT-4o — working from your photograph and your ten answers. No member of this house sees your picture.
  • It is sent over an encrypted connection to the service that reads it.
  • It is read once, for this assessment, and is not stored afterwards.
  • It is never attached to your email address. The analyser asks for no account and no payment — only somewhere to send the reading, so that you keep it.
  • It is read by the analysis service this house contracts, under an agreement that it is not retained and not used for training. Beyond that it is shared with nobody, never published, and never used for advertising.

The assessment describes the appearance of your skin and suggests a routine. It is not a medical diagnosis and it does not replace advice from a healthcare professional. If something on your skin concerns you medically, please see a doctor.

The consultation
    Question 1 of 10
    The Photograph

    One clear photograph.

    This is the half of the assessment you cannot answer in words. Add your photograph below — four things separate a useful reading from a guess, and they are worth the thirty seconds.

    Daylight, not lamplight

    Stand facing a window in the middle of the day. Overhead bulbs carve shadow into every line and read as texture that is not there.

    A bare face

    No make-up, no filter, no smoothing. Foundation is designed to hide precisely what the assessment is trying to see.

    Camera at eye level

    Straight to the lens, whole face in frame, hair back. Held high or low, the jaw and brow both distort.

    A resting face

    Relaxed, not smiling. Expression lines are the point of the exercise, and a smile hides half of them.

    No flash, no filters, no sunglasses, no heavy shadow across one side of the face. If you are unsure, take two and send the plainer one.
