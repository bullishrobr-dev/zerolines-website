The Zero Lines Protocol: A 6-Step System for Skin Longevity

Last updated: 13 May 2026 8 min read Free skin analysis · 4 min

Most skincare brands sell products. We built a protocol: a sequenced system where each formulation prepares the skin for the next, so the combined effect is greater than any single step could manage alone.

The Zero Lines Protocol emerged from a simple observation: the skin is not a passive surface that absorbs whatever you put on it. It's a dynamic, living organ with circadian rhythms, barrier defences, and cellular repair cycles. Throw random active ingredients at it, however potent each one is, and you get conflict rather than progress.

In short

  • Six formulations in sequence rather than six independent products: clear, signal, hydrate, protect, repair, maintain. Each step uses a different delivery mechanism, molecular weight and time of day.
  • Step one is a weekly enzymatic peeling gel rather than a daily scrub, so the surface is cleared without micro-tearing and everything after it lands on prepared skin.
  • The signalling steps are the Line Corrector and the Bio-Signal Face Serum — hydrolysed collagen peptides and Alpine botanical peptides, on a Pyrenean mineral water base.
  • The Day Cream carries no SPF and is not a sunscreen; sun protection goes over it every morning. Introduce the whole thing gradually — serum and day cream first, the peeling gel not before week seven.

Why Use a Protocol Instead of a Single Product?

Think of it like fitness. A single exercise, no matter how effective, won't transform your body. You need strength work, mobility, recovery, and nutrition, each supporting the others. Skincare works the same way.

Our six formulations are designed to work in sequence:

  1. Clear — Remove dead cells and congestion so actives can penetrate.
  2. Signal — Send molecular messages to fibroblasts and keratinocytes.
  3. Hydrate — Restore water balance and barrier integrity at the cellular level.
  4. Protect — Defend against UV, pollution, and oxidative stress during the day.
  5. Repair — Support the skin's natural overnight regeneration cycle.
  6. Maintain — Sustain results with accessible, consistent collagen support.

Each step uses a different delivery mechanism, a different molecular weight profile, and a different time of day. Together, they cover all four mechanisms of skin ageing we identified in our complete guide to skin ageing.

Step 1: Bio-Renewal Peeling Gel

Frequency: Once weekly, evening.

The foundation of any effective protocol is preparation. Dead skin cells accumulate on the surface, creating a barrier that blocks subsequent products from penetrating. Most people address this with daily scrubs, which create micro-tears in the skin, or with strong acids, which can over-exfoliate and compromise the barrier.

The Bio-Renewal Peeling Gel takes a gentler approach. Brown algae extract provides enzymatic exfoliation that dissolves the bonds between dead cells without abrasion. Aloe vera juice soothes as it works. And Pyrenean mineral water delivers trace minerals that support the skin's natural shedding cycle.

The result is smoother, more radiant skin that absorbs everything you apply afterwards. No redness, no rebound oiliness, none of the sensitivity that aggressive exfoliation causes.

The surface looks clearer and catches the light more evenly the same evening. Used once a week, it keeps the skin ready to receive everything that follows it in the protocol.

Step 2: Precision Collagen Renewal Line Corrector

Frequency: Daily, morning or evening.

This is the signalling step: the moment your skin receives the molecular instructions it needs to rebuild. The Line Corrector format is practical rather than decorative. It allows precise, hygienic dispensing of a highly concentrated formula without oxidation or contamination.

The key actives are hydrolysed collagen peptides small enough to reach the dermis, combined with botanical peptides derived from Alpine plants that have evolved to survive extreme environmental stress. These plant peptides signal collagen production, and they also support cellular resilience against UV and temperature damage.

It is formulated to support collagen synthesis over a sustained programme of use rather than to blur a line overnight. The intended difference is structural: skin that feels firmer, holds hydration better, and bounces back when pressed.

Step 3: Bio-Signal Face Serum

Frequency: Twice daily, after cleansing.

If the Line Corrector is the architect, the Bio-Signal Face Serum is the messenger. It's a high-velocity molecular delivery system that carries botanical extracts and minerals into the skin, where they can influence cellular behaviour.

The serum's base is Pyrenean mineral water. Not distilled, not filtered, but water that has percolated through granite and limestone at altitudes above 1,000 metres. That journey leaves it rich in magnesium, calcium, and silica, the elements that support enzymatic reactions in the skin and help maintain the ionic balance cells need to function.

Botanical extracts from Alpine edelweiss and gentian root provide antioxidant protection and support the skin's natural stress-response systems. The formula is light, fast-absorbing, and designed to layer cleanly under moisturiser without pilling or residue.

Step 4: Environmental Shield Day Cream

Six formulations in sequence rather than six independent products: clear, signal, hydrate, protect, repair, maintain. Each step uses a different delivery mechanism, molecular weight and time of day.

Frequency: Every morning.

Daytime skincare has one primary job: protection. UV radiation, pollution particulates, and blue light all generate free radicals that damage cellular DNA, break down collagen, and trigger inflammatory cascades. Moisturising is only half of a day cream's work. The other half is building a defensive layer.

The Environmental Shield Day Cream pairs red algae extract — a marine organism that has evolved its own defences against light and salt — with Pyrenean trace minerals, sweet almond oil and shea butter. It is built for the rest of the daytime load: pollution, dry air, temperature swings, the low-grade oxidative stress of an ordinary day outdoors.

It is not a sunscreen, and it carries no SPF rating. Sun protection goes on top of it, every morning, and nothing in this protocol replaces that. The texture is designed to make the habit easy — it absorbs quickly, sits under make-up, and takes a sunscreen over it without pilling.

Step 5: Renewal and Repair Night Cream

Frequency: Every evening.

Between 11 PM and 2 AM, your skin shifts into repair mode. Blood flow increases. Cellular turnover accelerates. Growth hormone peaks. This is when your skin is most receptive to reparative ingredients, and when a rich, nutrient-dense night cream delivers the greatest return.

The Renewal and Repair Night Cream is our richest formulation, built to support every aspect of overnight regeneration. Niacinamide (vitamin B3) strengthens the barrier, regulates sebum, and supports collagen synthesis. Hydrolysed collagen provides amino acid building blocks. Lavender floral water, a true hydrosol rather than synthetic fragrance, supports relaxation and sleep quality, which in turn gives skin repair its best conditions.

The cream forms a breathable occlusive layer that prevents trans-epidermal water loss without clogging pores. You wake up with skin that feels plump, smooth, and genuinely rested.

How to Start the Protocol: The Onboarding Sequence

You wouldn't run a marathon in new shoes without breaking them in first. Your skin is no different. Introducing all six formulations at once can overwhelm the barrier and trigger sensitivity or breakouts.

We recommend this onboarding sequence:

  • Weeks 1–2: Serum and Day Cream only. Establish hydration and protection.
  • Weeks 3–4: Add Night Cream. Introduce repair while you sleep.
  • Weeks 5–6: Add Line Corrector. Begin signalling for structural renewal.
  • Week 7 onwards: Add Peeling Gel (1x weekly). Clear the path for deeper penetration.

After 12 weeks, transition from the Line Corrector to the Refill for maintenance.

Every skin is different, and the skin analysis can personalise this sequence based on your specific concerns, climate, and current routine. But the principle remains: introduce gradually, respect the barrier, and let biology do what biology does best.
