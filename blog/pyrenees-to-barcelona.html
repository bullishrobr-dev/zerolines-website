From Pyrenees to Barcelona: Tracing Zero Lines' Alpine Ingredients

Last updated: 13 May 2026 6 min read Free skin analysis · 2 min

Every bottle of Zero Lines carries ingredients that have travelled nearly 200 kilometres, from a single spring high in the Andorran Pyrenees to our formulation laboratory in Barcelona. The distance is not a logistics detail. It is the point.

In short

  • The water surfaces at 1,834 metres in a protected valley with no agriculture, industry or permanent settlement within five kilometres, after an estimated eighteen to twenty-two years underground.
  • It is collected through gravity-fed channels rather than pumped, then held at 4°C in insulated stainless tankers for the 185 kilometres to Barcelona, arriving within 48 hours of leaving the spring.
  • Barcelona was chosen for its manufacturing heritage, its port, and a regulatory environment that holds every batch to Spanish and EU standards. The laboratory is a converted textile warehouse in Poblenou.
  • Every batch is to be checked for mineral consistency, peptide integrity by HPLC and retained bioactivity. That is the specification the house has written for itself, and nothing has been released against it yet.

Where the Water Comes From: 1,800 Metres Above Sea Level

Our primary mineral water source emerges at 1,834 metres, in a protected valley with no agriculture, no industry and no permanent settlement within a 5-kilometre radius. The water has spent an estimated 18–22 years underground, percolating through granite and limestone, and it surfaces cold and heavy with dissolved minerals.

We collect it through gravity-fed channels that have been in place for decades rather than by pumping, which can disturb aquifer pressure and alter the mineral equilibrium. Flow is monitored continuously so we never take more than the spring naturally recharges.

How Cold-Chain Transport Protects Mineral Water

Within six hours of collection, the water is transferred to insulated stainless steel tankers maintained at 4°C. It travels 185 kilometres to our Barcelona facility, arriving within 48 hours of leaving the spring.

This cold chain matters. Warm water loses dissolved gases and can precipitate minerals. By keeping the water cold throughout transport, we preserve the exact mineral profile that makes it effective for skincare.

Why We Formulate in Barcelona

We chose Barcelona as our manufacturing base for reasons that go beyond convenience. The city's pharmaceutical and cosmetic manufacturing heritage dates back to the 19th century. Its port provides access to marine ingredients from the Mediterranean. And its regulatory environment, governed by both Spanish and EU cosmetic directives, holds every batch to strict standards of safety and transparency.

Our laboratory in the Poblenou district occupies a converted textile warehouse, a piece of Barcelona's industrial past put to scientific use. Inside, the facility runs to pharmaceutical-grade standards: HEPA-filtered air, positive-pressure clean rooms and full batch traceability from raw material to finished product.

Alpine Botanicals in Skincare: Edelweiss and Gentian

The Pyrenean connection extends beyond the mineral water. These are the plants of that ecosystem, and the ones we are formulating with — the finished ingredient list will be published on each product page when there is a batch to publish it against:

  • Edelweiss extract: Harvested from cultivated Alpine gardens at 1,500 metres, this flower has evolved potent antioxidant compounds to survive extreme UV at altitude, which is what draws formulators to it.
  • Gentian root: A bitter root used in traditional Pyrenean medicine for centuries. The compounds behind that reputation are being studied; a long history of use is a reason to look, not a substitute for evidence.
  • Lavender floral water: While not from the Pyrenees specifically, our lavender hydrosol comes from organic fields in the Provence Alps, processed using traditional steam distillation.

How a Zero Lines Formula Is Developed

Raw ingredients are only the beginning. The craft of formulation lies in combining them so they work with each other rather than against each other. This is where our four years of development becomes visible.

Each Zero Lines product went through between 40 and 80 iterations before reaching its final form. The variables were endless: pH, viscosity, emulsion stability, skin feel, absorption rate, compatibility with the other products in the protocol, and shelf life under various temperature conditions.

The result is a system where the BioSignal Serum prepares the skin for the Line Corrector, which in turn creates the optimal conditions for the Day Cream or Night Cream to seal and protect. Each step was designed with the others in mind.

Quality Control: From Batch to Bottle

Every batch will go through the same quality control before release: microbiological testing, stability testing at accelerated temperatures, pH verification, viscosity measurement and a sensory check by experienced assessors.

And it goes further than standard cosmetic QC. Every batch is to be tested for mineral consistency in the water base, for peptide integrity by HPLC — confirming the signal peptides have not degraded during formulation — and by in-vitro assay for retained bioactivity. That is the specification the house has written for itself. Nothing has been released against it yet, because nothing has been released.

Why We Use Airless Packaging

Packaging is part of the product, not an afterthought. Airless pumps prevent oxidation of sensitive actives like peptides and antioxidants. Opaque containers shield light-sensitive ingredients from UV degradation. And our glass bottles are fully recyclable, with labels printed using soy-based inks.

The Line Corrector uses a dual-chamber design that keeps the active peptide solution separate from the hydrating base until the moment of application. Conventional packaging cannot match that. It is harder to make, and it is the only way we know to keep those peptides at full strength until they reach your skin.
