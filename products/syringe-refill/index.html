01 What It Is

Not a different product. The same one, continued.

The Refill is not a lighter version, a starter size or a variant. It is the identical Advanced Skin Correction treatment — the same hydrolysed collagen, the same botanical extract complex, the same botanical signal peptides — delivered in a continuation cartridge rather than a complete unit.

The Precision Collagen Activation Syringe carries the precision applicator: the engineered part, the piece that makes targeted delivery possible at all. That applicator is designed to be kept. The Refill is simply what goes into it next.

The device is the instrument. The cartridge is the treatment.

02 Designed To Support

What the cartridge is built to do.

Written as intent, not as outcome. These are the things the formulation is designed to support — the reasons it exists in this form.

  • Allows the Advanced Skin Correction treatment to continue without interruption between cycles.

  • Carries the same hydrolysed collagen and botanical peptide complex as the original Syringe.

  • Formulated for weekly treatment continuation, and for as-needed application before an occasion.

  • Supports sustained, long-term adherence to the protocol rather than a single course.

  • An easily replaceable cartridge, so continuity never depends on remembering a procedure.

  • Requires the Precision Collagen Activation Syringe applicator — it is not a standalone formulation.

03 How It Works

The mechanism does not reset.

When the original cartridge is spent, it is removed and the Refill takes its place. The precision tip is retained, so the delivery characteristics of the treatment — how much, how deep, how precisely placed — are unchanged from the first application to the last.

Inside, the formulation is identical: the dual-action hydrolysed collagen and botanical peptide complex, designed to work on the surface for immediate visible refinement while the signalling components address cellular communication over a longer horizon. Two timescales, one cartridge.

That is the whole logic of the refill system. Continuity is the active ingredient; a protocol interrupted is a protocol that starts again.

Requires the Syringe applicator · External use only

The Refill is designed exclusively for use with the Zero Lines precision applicator and cannot be used on its own. For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs, and keep out of reach of children.

04 Application Protocol

The same protocol as the Syringe. Nothing to relearn.

Two approaches, and they are meant to coexist: a weekly application that builds, and an occasional one that shows.

  1. 01

    Replace the cartridge

    Twist the depleted cartridge off the applicator base. Seat the Refill and twist until secure. Under thirty seconds, and no tools.

  2. 02

    Weekly treatment

    Once a week, on the expression lines you are actually working on. Same precision, same targeted delivery, same formulation as before.

  3. 03

    As-needed application

    Before an occasion, apply thirty to sixty minutes ahead. The surface-level peptide support is designed to hold through the day.

  4. 04

    Continue

    Then keep going. The Refill exists so that consistency is the default state of the protocol rather than an act of discipline.

05 The Ritual

Four movements. Under a minute.

The change itself is deliberately unremarkable. It should feel like replacing an ink cartridge in a good pen — quiet, certain, over.

  1. 1

    Remove

    Twist the empty cartridge off the handle and set it aside.

  2. 2

    Insert

    Align the new cartridge with the base. It seats with a single click.

  3. 3

    Prime

    One pump to draw the formulation through the tip. Now it is ready.

  4. 4

    Continue

    Return to the weekly rhythm. Nothing has restarted; it simply carries on.

06 Questions

Asked and answered.

Why does the Refill exist alongside the Syringe?

The Syringe includes the precision applicator — the device that makes targeted delivery possible. The Refill is the treatment cartridge alone. Because the applicator is retained and reused, nothing about the mechanism has to be remade every cycle, which is better for the environment and makes long-term continuation straightforward.

Is the formulation exactly the same?

Yes. The Refill carries the same hydrolysed collagen and botanical peptide complex as the original Syringe. There is no difference in concentration, quality or intended outcome — only the packaging.

Can the Refill be used without the Syringe?

No. The cartridge requires the Precision Collagen Activation Syringe applicator in order to function and is not a standalone formulation. The Syringe comes first; the Refill continues it.

How long does one Refill last?

One cartridge is designed to deliver approximately sixty applications. At once-weekly use that is roughly a year of continuous protocol. Where as-needed application is added before events, the duration will be shorter.

How will I know when to replace it?

Plan the next cartridge when about two weeks of formulation remain. The protocol depends on continuity, and the point of the Refill is that there is never a gap in it.

What if the applicator is damaged?

The precision applicator is engineered to be durable. In the rare event of damage, a replacement Syringe unit would be required in order to continue using Refills.

How many times can the applicator be reused?

It is designed for repeated cartridge changes. With ordinary care it should carry many refill cycles across years of use — that longevity is the reason the system was built in two parts.

Why does the protocol begin with the Syringe rather than the Refill?

The Syringe is the entrance to the system: the applicator itself, and the introduction to how the treatment is applied. The Refill assumes that groundwork. It is written for someone already inside the protocol.
