01 The Formulation

A different approach to overnight care.

Renewal and Repair Night Cream is not a heavier version of the Environmental Shield Day Cream. It is a specialised regenerative formulation, built for a different job entirely — to work alongside the skin’s own overnight renewal, rather than to shield it from a day it is no longer having.

The texture is richer, the base is lavender floral water rather than a protective emulsion, and the actives are chosen for rebuilding rather than defence. It goes on last, and it is still working at dawn.

03 The Mechanism

The skin does its best work in the dark.

During sleep the skin’s renewal processes are at their most active. Blood flow rises, cell turnover accelerates, and the tissue moves out of defence and into repair. Renewal and Repair Night Cream is formulated for that window rather than around it.

Hydrolysed collagen and a shea butter complex do the structural work together — the collagen fragments small enough to read as signal, the butter holding water in the tissue across six to eight hours rather than releasing it in the first one. Niacinamide is included to support barrier function, and lavender floral water carries the formulation. The base, as with everything we make, is Pyrenean mineral water.

We are not the repair. We are the instructions.

External use only

Renewal and Repair Night Cream is formulated exclusively for external application to the face and neck. Avoid contact with the eyes. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.

04 Application Protocol

Last on. Longest on.

The night cream closes the evening sequence. It seals the layers beneath it — which is why it follows the BioSignal Facial Serum rather than the other way round.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Last step of the evening, after the serum. Slightly more than you would use in the morning — roughly an almond-sized amount for face and neck.

  2. 02

    Massage

    Upward strokes, face then neck. Take the time — this is the part of the protocol that is meant to feel like something.

  3. 03

    Absorb

    Five to ten minutes before lying down, so the cream finishes in the skin rather than on the pillow.

  4. 04

    Sleep

    Six to eight hours. The formulation stays with the skin for the whole of the repair window, and is still there at dawn.

06 Questions

Eight questions, answered.

Why do I need a separate night cream?

The skin’s needs differ between day and night. Through the day it is largely defending itself — against light, pollution, temperature. Overnight it shifts into repair, and cell turnover accelerates. Renewal and Repair Night Cream is formulated for the second of those states rather than the first.

Can I use the Environmental Shield Day Cream at night instead?

You can, but you would be applying environmental protection you do not need while asleep, and leaving out the ingredients chosen for the overnight window. For the protocol as it is designed, use the evening formulation in the evening.

Will it mark my pillowcase?

It is formulated to absorb fully within five to ten minutes. Apply it at the start of your evening wind-down rather than as you get into bed, and it will have settled long before you lie down.

How long does one bottle last?

At an almond-sized amount nightly, the 50 ml bottle is designed to last roughly two to three months.

Is it too rich for oily skin?

It is richer than the day cream by design — skin tolerates, and generally benefits from, more nourishment overnight. If your skin is very oily, start with less than an almond-sized amount and calibrate upwards over a fortnight.

Can I use it alongside retinol?

The Longevity Protocol is designed as a complete system, so the simplest answer is to move across to it. If you would rather keep a retinol you are settled with, alternate nights rather than layering the two, and introduce the night cream on the off nights first.

What is the circadian renewal cycle?

Skin runs on a 24-hour rhythm. In daylight it is largely in defence — barrier, pigment, antioxidant response. Overnight, blood flow rises and cell turnover accelerates as the tissue moves into repair. The night cream is formulated for that second phase.

Should I apply it to my neck as well?

Yes. Neck skin is thinner than facial skin and typically shows structural change earlier. Carry the cream down the neck and across the décolletage using the same upward strokes.
