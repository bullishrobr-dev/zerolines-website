02 The Mechanism

The skin does its best work in the dark.

This is not a heavier version of the Environmental Shield Day Cream. It is built for a different job entirely. Through daylight the skin is largely defending itself; overnight, blood flow rises, cell turnover accelerates and the tissue moves out of defence and into repair. The formulation is timed to that second state.

Hydrolysed collagen is carried in a restorative shea butter lipid matrix — the collagen in fragments small enough to read as signal, the lipids holding water in the skin for the length of a night rather than the length of an hour. Niacinamide supports barrier recovery. Lavender floral water carries the whole of it, on a base of Pyrenean mineral water.

We are not the repair. We are the instructions.

05 Questions

Six questions, answered.

Will I actually notice anything the first morning?

That is the effect the cream is built around. It is formulated to hold water in the tissue across the whole night rather than release it in the first hour, so skin tends to look plumper and more rested at dawn — visibly, from the very first morning. Zero Lines is in pre-launch, so this describes how the formulation is designed to behave rather than a measured result, and skin differs from person to person.

Why do I need a separate night cream?

Because the skin is doing a different job at night. Through the day it is largely defending itself against light, pollution and temperature. Overnight it moves into repair and cell turnover accelerates. This is formulated for the second of those states rather than the first.

Will it mark my pillowcase?

Not if you give it a few minutes. It is formulated to absorb within five to ten. Apply it at the start of your evening wind-down rather than as you get into bed and it will have settled long before you lie down.

Is it too rich for oily skin?

It is richer than the day cream deliberately — skin generally tolerates, and benefits from, more nourishment overnight. If yours is very oily, start with less than an almond-sized amount and calibrate upward across a fortnight.

Can I keep using my retinol?

The Longevity Protocol is designed as a complete system, so the simplest answer is to move across to it. If you would rather keep a retinol you are settled with, alternate nights rather than layering the two, and introduce the night cream on the off nights first.

Should I take it down the neck?

Yes. Neck skin is thinner than facial skin and usually shows structural change earlier. Carry the cream down the neck and across the décolletage with the same upward strokes.
