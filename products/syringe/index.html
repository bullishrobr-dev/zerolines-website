02 How It Works

Not a cream. Not a serum. A placement.

Twist the base and a measured dose arrives at a narrow tip. You place it on the few square centimetres where expression has settled — between the brows, at the outer eye, above the lip. A moisturiser covers and a serum carries; this one places. Nothing is spread across the face, and nothing is wasted.

Two things then happen from the same dose. At the surface, botanical signal peptides carried on rose and chamomile flower waters work on how the skin reads — smoother, more even, light falling differently across a line. Below it, hydrolysed collagen is broken into fragments small enough to be read as a signal rather than sit as a film, working with the skin’s own repair and renewal signalling.

04 How To Use It

Four movements, once a week.

  1. 1

    Cleanse

    Cleanse, then dry the face completely. Nothing goes on damp skin.

  2. 2

    Dispense

    Twist the base once. One measured dose is the whole application.

  3. 3

    Place

    Along the lines themselves: brow, outer eye, forehead, above the lip.

  4. 4

    Press

    Tap with a fingertip until absorbed. Do not rub. Then leave it a week.

Weekly — for the lasting effect

One evening a week, in the same order, is what builds the structural half. Repetition is the whole of it; a protocol is only a protocol because it repeats.

Before an occasion — for the immediate one

Apply 30 to 60 minutes ahead and the surface phase does its work in time. Use it as often as you want this half. Never more than once in a single day.

06 Questions

Six things worth asking.

Is there a needle? Is anything injected?

No. Nothing is injected and there is no needle. The applicator borrows the shape of a clinical instrument for one reason: accuracy. You twist the base, a measured dose arrives at the narrow tip, and you place it on the surface of the skin. It is a cream-based formulation for external facial use only.

What will I notice the first time I use it?

The immediate effect. Applied along expression lines, the skin is designed to look visibly smoother within minutes and to hold that through the day. The lasting effect is not something you see on day one — it is built through consistent weekly use. How quickly this shows will differ from person to person.

How often should I use it?

Once a week for the lasting effect. Then as often as you want the immediate one — apply 30 to 60 minutes before an occasion. Do not apply more than once in a day.

Why is there a Refill?

Because the immediate effect is worth having on its own. Some people will only ever want lines to look smoother on the day, the way they would reach for makeup. The Refill cartridge carries the same formulation, fits the applicator you already own, and lets that continue without buying the instrument again.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It is formulated for external facial application and intended to suit most skin types. If your skin is sensitive, patch test on the inner forearm first. Keep it away from the eye itself, from mucous membranes and from broken skin. If irritation occurs, stop using it and speak to a healthcare professional.

What happens if I stop using it?

The immediate effect is temporary: it fades as the application wears off, in the same way makeup does. The support built through consistent weekly use is designed to persist for a period afterwards and then gradually diminish. Individual experience will vary.
