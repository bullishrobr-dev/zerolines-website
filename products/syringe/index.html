01 What It Is

A new category of skin treatment.

It is not a cream. It is not a serum. The Precision Collagen Activation Syringe is an advanced skin-correction treatment — a concentrated formulation held in a precision applicator and placed, deliberately, on the few square centimetres that actually need it.

03 How It Works

Two phases, one dose.

On application, the botanical peptide complex begins at the surface, where it is designed to support the look of smoother, more refined skin. This is the phase intended to be visible soonest, and to hold through the day.

Beneath it, hydrolysed collagen works at the level of the skin’s own repair and renewal signalling. This phase is slower by design. It is built to accumulate with consistent weekly application — supporting longer-term change in texture, firmness and overall appearance rather than a single evening’s effect.

Slower to show. Structural when it does.

Carried on rose and chamomile flower waters. The surface phase — the half of the dual action designed to be seen first.

External use only

The Precision Collagen Activation Syringe is designed exclusively for external facial application. No injection is required or intended. Do not ingest, and do not apply to mucous membranes or broken skin. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.

04 Application Protocol

Two ways to use it.

The Syringe follows a structured correction protocol: once weekly for the long, structural work — and as needed, in the hours before an occasion, for the surface refinement.

See the full protocol
  1. 01

    Weekly treatment

    For the structural half of the protocol, apply once a week. Twist the base to dispense a measured amount to the tip, then place it directly on expression lines and areas of visible concern.

  2. 02

    As-needed refinement

    Before an occasion, apply 30 to 60 minutes beforehand. The surface-phase peptides are designed to support a more refined look that carries through the day.

  3. 03

    Precision targeting

    Place directly on expression lines: forehead lines, crow’s feet, the frown lines between the brows, and the fine lines above and below the lip. The tip exists to keep the dose exactly where it is intended.

  4. 04

    Gentle activation

    Pat the treated areas with a fingertip to support absorption. Do not rub. Allow one to two minutes before the next step in the protocol.

06 Questions

Eight things worth asking.

Is this an injectable treatment?

No. The Precision Collagen Activation Syringe is a non-injectable, external application treatment. The applicator resembles a syringe so the formulation can be placed precisely, but no needle and no injection are involved. It is a cream-based formulation designed for targeted external use.

Will I see results from the first use?

The Syringe is built around a dual-action approach. The surface phase is designed to support a visibly smoother, more refined look soon after application; the deeper phase is designed to build progressively with consistent weekly use. Zero Lines is in pre-launch, so nothing here has been measured in a trial and no result is being claimed. Individual experience will vary.

How often should I use it?

Once a week for the structural, long-term half of the protocol. For a more immediate refinement before an occasion, apply 30 to 60 minutes beforehand. Do not use more than once in a day.

How does it differ from a regular cream or serum?

It sits in a different category. A moisturiser hydrates the surface and a serum carries actives across the whole face. This is an advanced skin-correction treatment placed only on specific areas, formulated to support the skin’s own collagen production and the appearance of eased expression and fluid balance exactly where it is placed.

What is the Refill, and why does the Syringe come first?

The Syringe is the key to the Zero Lines ecosystem — the applicator is the instrument the whole protocol runs on. The Refill cartridge carries the same concentrated formulation and is engineered exclusively for that applicator, which is how the protocol continues once the first cartridge is spent.

How long does one Syringe last?

One Syringe holds approximately sixty applications. At once-weekly use that is designed to cover roughly twelve months. Additional as-needed use will shorten that.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

The formulation is designed for external facial application and is intended to suit most skin types. If your skin is sensitive, patch test on the inner forearm first. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.

What happens if I stop using it?

The immediate surface refinement is temporary and fades when use stops. The progressive effect that builds with consistent weekly use is designed to persist for a period afterwards and then gradually diminish. Individual experience will vary.
