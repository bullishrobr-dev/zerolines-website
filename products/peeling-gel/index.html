01 The Formulation

A different approach to exfoliation.

There are no abrasive particles in this formulation, and nothing about the way it works depends on friction. A scrub removes what it can reach by force, and takes a little of the barrier with it every time. That is a trade the protocol is not willing to make once a week for the rest of your life.

Instead, the gel loosens the bonds holding spent cells to the surface and rinses away with them. What is left is not a polished face. It is a receptive one — and receptivity is the entire point of standing first in a six-step sequence.

Step one clears the canvas. It does not paint on it.

Weekly by design

A structured step

A defined exfoliation step applied once a week, written to work progressively — supporting natural renewal without over-processing the skin that has to live with it.

Barrier first

Gel, not grit

A gel-based system that works with the skin’s own chemistry rather than against it — formulated to exfoliate without compromising the protective barrier.

Sequence

03 Mechanism

Chemistry, not abrasion.

The formulation uses a citric acid complex to loosen the bonds holding spent cells to the outermost layer, so that they release in the ordinary course of the skin’s own shedding cycle rather than being scrubbed off it.

It works within minutes. Rinse thoroughly at the end of the contact time and continue straight into the next step — freshly cleared skin is at its most receptive in the minutes that follow.

External use only

The Peeling Gel is formulated exclusively for external facial application. Avoid contact with the eyes and mucous membranes. If irritation occurs, rinse immediately and discontinue use. Consult a healthcare professional if irritation persists.

04 Application Protocol

Once a week. Two minutes.

Consistency and timing are most of it. The interval is not a suggestion printed on the tube — it is part of how the formulation was designed to behave.

  1. 01

    Weekly application

    Apply to cleansed, dry skin once a week. Massage gently in circular motions until the gel is spread evenly across the face.

  2. 02

    Contact time

    Leave for up to two minutes. Do not exceed it — longer is not better, and the limit is deliberate.

  3. 03

    Rinse and continue

    Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water, then go straight on to step two — the collagen syringe — and the serum after it.

  4. 04

    Consistent scheduling

    Use on the same day each week. Most people prefer the evening, so the skin rests overnight before it meets the day.

Step one runs on one evening a week. The syringe follows it within minutes, and the daily steps — serum, then day or night cream — carry the instruction through the rest of the week.

05 The Ritual

One evening a week.

Four movements, six minutes, fifty-two times a year. The protocol asks for very little of your week — it only asks for the same evening, every week.

  1. 01 Clean skin
    Freshly cleansed, patted dry. Nothing else on the face.
  2. 02 Massage
    Circular motions for sixty seconds, forehead to jaw.
  3. 03 Wait
    Up to two minutes. No longer, however tempting.
  4. 04 Rinse
    Lukewarm water, pat dry, and straight on to step two.
06 Questions

Before you begin.

How often should I use the Peeling Gel?

Once a week. The protocol is written around a weekly interval, which is designed to support the skin’s own renewal without over-processing it. More often is not more effective, and it risks the barrier this formulation is built to protect.

Can I use it alongside other exfoliating products?

We would not. Keep other exfoliants off the skin on the day you use this one. The protocol is designed as a complete system — let this step do its work, then continue into the treatment steps that follow it.

Will it make my skin more sensitive to the sun?

As with any exfoliation, skin can be more sensitive to ultraviolet light in the days that follow. Sun protection every morning is part of the protocol in any case, and it matters most in the day or two after this step.

What should I apply afterwards?

Continue straight into the sequence: the Precision Collagen Activation Syringe, then the BioSignal Facial Serum. Freshly cleared skin is at its most receptive, which is precisely why this step comes first.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It is formulated to be gentle — a skin-compatible pH, soothing botanical extracts, and no abrasive particles anywhere in it. If your skin is particularly reactive, patch test on the inner forearm before the first full application.

How long does one tube last?

Used once weekly as directed, one tube is designed to last approximately three to four months.

Can I skip this step in the protocol?

You can, but the sequence is doing real work. This step clears the surface so that what follows meets living tissue rather than the residue of the week behind it. For the protocol to behave as it was designed to, begin here.

What should I expect?

A surface that feels smoother to the touch once it is rinsed. Beyond that, this is a protocol measured in months rather than days: the texture and clarity it is designed to support build with consistent weekly use, and every skin arrives at its own pace.
