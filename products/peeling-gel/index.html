02 How it works

Chemistry, not abrasion.

There is not one abrasive particle in this formulation, and nothing about the way it works depends on friction. A scrub takes what it can reach by force, and a little of the barrier with it every time. Once a week, for years, that is a poor trade.

Instead a citric acid complex loosens the bonds holding spent cells at the surface, so they leave with the water when you rinse. Aloe vera juice carries it and keeps the skin comfortable while it works. The whole thing stops the moment it goes down the sink.

Step one clears the canvas. It does not paint on it.

External use only

Formulated exclusively for external application to facial skin. Avoid contact with the eyes and mucous membranes. If irritation occurs, rinse immediately and discontinue use. Consult a healthcare professional if irritation persists. Keep out of reach of children.

06 Questions

Before you begin.

What will I notice, and when?

Two things, on two different clocks. Immediately: once you rinse, the surface feels smoother and looks brighter that same evening. Over time: with a weekly rhythm the surface stays clear, and the appearance of texture refines across the weeks. Every skin arrives at its own pace.

How often should I use the Peeling Gel?

Once a week. The protocol is written around a weekly interval, designed to support the skin’s own renewal without over-processing it. More often is not more effective, and it risks the barrier this formulation is built to protect.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It is formulated to be gentle: a skin-compatible pH, soothing botanical extracts, and no abrasive particles anywhere in it. If your skin is particularly reactive, patch test on the inner forearm before the first full application.

Does it make skin more sensitive to sunlight?

As with any exfoliation, skin can be more sensitive to ultraviolet light in the days that follow. Sun protection every morning is part of the protocol in any case, and it matters most in the day or two after this step.

What do I apply afterwards?

Continue straight into the sequence: the Precision Collagen Activation Syringe, then the BioSignal Facial Serum. Freshly cleared skin is at its most receptive, which is precisely why this step stands first.

Can I use it alongside my other exfoliants?

We would keep them off the skin on the day you use this one. The protocol is designed as a complete system: let this step do its work, then continue into the treatment steps that follow it.
