01 The Formulation

A different category of daily treatment.

The Serum is not a standard hydrator, and it is not a vitamin C drop. It is a concentrated daily treatment built to carry bioactive compounds to the depth at which renewal actually happens — and to keep carrying them, every day, for as long as the protocol runs.

Everything in it sits on a base of mineral water drawn from high-altitude Pyrenean springs: zinc, selenium, copper and magnesium in ionic balance, the cofactors the skin’s own antioxidant enzymes need in order to work at all.

Key ingredients

  • Botanical peptide complex — derived from watermelon extract, formulated to resemble the skin’s own signalling molecules.
  • Hydrolysed collagen — broken to a molecular weight chosen for absorption, not for surface film.
  • Pyrenean mineral water base — the carrier, and the reason the minerals arrive in ionic form.

03 Mechanism

How the signal travels.

The Serum carries a botanical peptide complex formulated to resemble the messenger molecules the skin already uses. Those peptides are intended to reach the fibroblasts — the cells responsible for building collagen — and to support the processes involved in synthesis and tissue repair.

Hydrolysed collagen travels with them, cut to a molecular weight chosen for absorption rather than for surface feel. Together the two are designed to support structural renewal, which is why the effect is progressive: the tissue does the work, on its own timetable.

External use only

The Serum is formulated exclusively for external application to the face and neck. Avoid contact with the eyes. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.

04 Application Protocol

Consistency is the active ingredient.

Four instructions, repeated. Cellular support is cumulative — the rhythm matters far more than the quantity, and more than any single application ever will.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Three to four drops onto cleansed face and neck, morning and evening. Twice daily is the rhythm the protocol is written for.

  2. 02

    Press

    Press the serum in with your fingertips rather than rubbing. Pressing supports absorption without pulling or stretching the skin.

  3. 03

    Wait

    Give it about a minute before the day or night cream. The actives need the head start; the cream then seals the work in.

  4. 04

    Continue

    Renewal is gradual. Eight to twelve weeks of unbroken use is the horizon the protocol is designed around.

06 Questions

Frequently asked.

How long before I see results?

The Serum is designed for progressive support rather than an overnight change. Texture and radiance are the first qualities the formulation is built to address; the structural support it is designed for accumulates across eight to twelve weeks of consistent twice-daily use. Individual results vary.

Can I use it alongside other serums?

The Serum is designed as the comprehensive daily treatment inside the Longevity Protocol, so we recommend it as your primary active serum. If you want to keep another product in your routine, use it at a different time of day.

Should I use it in the morning or the evening?

The protocol is written for twice daily — morning and evening. If you prefer once, choose the evening, so the active compounds work alongside the skin’s own overnight renewal cycle.

How long does one bottle last?

With twice-daily use of three to four drops, one bottle is designed to last approximately six to eight weeks.

Is it oily or heavy?

No. The texture is lightweight and fast-absorbing, formulated to leave no residue so that it layers cleanly under a moisturiser or a sunscreen.

Can I skip this step if I use the Precision Collagen Activation Syringe?

They do different jobs. The Syringe is a weekly, targeted intervention on specific areas. The Serum is the daily treatment across the whole face and neck. The protocol is designed around both, in sequence.

Is it suitable for all skin types?

The Serum is formulated for external facial application and is suitable for most skin types, including oily, combination and dry. If your skin is reactive, or you are treating a diagnosed condition, speak to your doctor or a dermatologist before adding any new active.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is undone. Consistency is what the protocol is built on, so simply return to the routine the next day — regular daily application is what the progressive support is designed around.
