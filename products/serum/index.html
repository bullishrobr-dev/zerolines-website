02 Mechanism

Why both things happen.

The same-day change is a surface story, and we would rather say so. The mineral base and the humectants leave the skin holding water more evenly within minutes — and skin that holds water evenly reflects light evenly, so it reads smoother and more luminous. Nothing structural has happened yet.

The slower change is a different mechanism entirely. The botanical peptide complex is shaped to resemble the messenger molecules the skin already uses, and hydrolysed collagen travels with it at a molecular weight chosen for absorption. Together they are formulated to support the fibroblast activity behind the skin’s own structure.

Which is why it is progressive: the tissue does the work, on its own timetable. Daily application simply keeps the instruction arriving.

We are not the repair. We are the instructions.

04 How to Use It

Ninety seconds, twice a day.

Rhythm matters far more than quantity here. More drops will not speed anything up; a missed fortnight is what actually costs you.

  1. 01

    Cleanse

    Start on clean skin, still barely damp. It travels better into skin that is not bone dry.

  2. 02

    Warm

    Three to four drops into the palm. Press the hands together before they touch your face.

  3. 03

    Press

    Press it in — face, then neck, then décolletage. Never drag. Pressing helps absorption without pulling the skin.

  4. 04

    Layer

    Give it about a minute, then the day cream or the night cream, according to the hour.

06 Questions

Frequently asked.

What will I notice on the first day?

It absorbs in seconds, and the skin looks plumper and more luminous almost straight away — often before you have finished the rest of your routine. That is a change in appearance, and it is meant to be one.

How long before the lasting effect shows?

Daily use is what carries it. The support accumulates gradually, and eight to twelve weeks of unbroken twice-daily use is the horizon the protocol is written for. Individual results vary.

Morning or evening?

Both — the protocol is written for twice daily. If you can only manage once, choose the evening, so the actives sit alongside the skin’s own overnight renewal.

Can I keep using my other serum?

The BioSignal is designed as the primary active serum inside the protocol. If you want to keep another, use it at a different time of day rather than layering the two.

Do I still need the Syringe if I use this?

They do different jobs. The Syringe is a weekly, targeted treatment on specific expression lines; the Serum is the daily one, across the whole face and neck. The protocol is designed around both.

Is it suitable for reactive skin?

It is formulated for external use on the face, neck and décolletage and suits most skin types, including oily, combination and dry. If your skin is reactive, or you are treating a diagnosed condition, speak to your doctor or a dermatologist before adding any new active.
