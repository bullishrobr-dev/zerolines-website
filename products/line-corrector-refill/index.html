02 What It Is

Not another product. The same one, continued.

The Refill is a cartridge. What it does not carry is the applicator — the engineered part, the precision tip that places a measured dose on a few square centimetres rather than a whole face. That is designed to be kept; the Refill is what goes into it next.

Which is the honest reason it exists. Some people will only ever want the immediate effect — the line that looks softer by the evening, the face that photographs better that day. That is a perfectly legitimate way to use this range, and the Refill is how you do it indefinitely: the applicator is bought once, and after that you replace only what has been used.

The applicator is the instrument. The cartridge is the treatment.

04 How To Use It

Change it. Then carry on exactly as before.

  1. 01

    Change the cartridge

    Twist the spent cartridge off the base, seat the Refill, twist until it locks. One turn of the base primes the tip. Under a minute, no tools.

  2. 02

    Weekly, as before

    Once a week, on clean dry skin, placed directly on the lines you are working on. Pat gently; do not rub.

  3. 03

    Or before an occasion

    Thirty to sixty minutes ahead, when what you want is the immediate effect to carry the evening.

External use only

For external facial application; no injection is required or intended. Avoid the eyes, broken skin and mucous membranes, and keep out of reach of children. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Continuity

The ritual continues.

A spring does not begin again each morning; it simply keeps running, and that is the whole of its usefulness. The Refill lets the protocol behave the same way — the applicator stays where you left it, the formulation is replaced, and between one cartridge and the next nothing about the ritual changes.

07 Questions

Six worth asking.

Can I use the Refill on its own?

No. The Refill is a cartridge, not a complete product; it requires the Precision Collagen Renewal Line Corrector applicator to work. The Line Corrector is bought once, the Refill afterwards.

Is the formulation identical to the Line Corrector's?

Yes. The same hydrolysed collagen, botanical signal peptides and botanical extract complex, on the same Pyrenean mineral water base. Nothing is diluted or simplified — only the packaging differs.

What if I only want the immediate effect?

A perfectly legitimate way to use the range — and the Refill is the affordable way to keep doing it. Because the applicator is reused rather than replaced, continuing indefinitely costs a fraction of starting again.

How long does one cartridge last?

Approximately sixty applications. At the once-weekly rhythm that is roughly twelve months; if you also apply it before occasions, sooner.

How do I change the cartridge?

Twist the spent cartridge off the base, seat the new one and twist until it locks, then turn the base once to prime the tip. Under a minute, no tools.
