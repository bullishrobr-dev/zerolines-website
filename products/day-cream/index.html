01 The Formulation

A different approach to daytime protection.

Environmental Shield Day Cream is not a basic moisturiser. It is a protective formulation, written to shield the skin from the aggressors it meets between leaving the house and coming home — particulate pollution, oxidative stress, the dehydrating dryness of air conditioning and central heating.

Sweet almond oil and shea butter carry red algae and cucumber extracts over a Pyrenean mineral complex of zinc, selenium and copper. Those minerals are not decoration: they are the cofactors the skin’s own antioxidant enzymes need in order to work at all.

03 How It Works

A breathable film. A mineral cofactor.

The cream forms a breathable protective film across the surface of the skin. That film is designed to do two things at once: hold environmental particles at a distance, and slow the water loss that makes skin feel tight by mid-afternoon.

Beneath it, the Pyrenean mineral water works at skin level to support the enzymatic defence systems already there. Zinc, selenium and copper are essential cofactors for the antioxidant enzymes that neutralise the free radicals generated by UV exposure and pollution — the enzymes exist regardless; the minerals are what let them run.

Not a sunscreen

Environmental Shield Day Cream is designed to support the skin’s defence against environmental stressors. It is not a substitute for sunscreen and carries no SPF rating. Apply a broad-spectrum SPF once the cream has set. External use only. Avoid the eye area, and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

04 Application Protocol

Four movements, every morning.

The day cream is the protective step: it seals in the treatment layers beneath it and prepares the skin for everything the day will ask of it. It follows the serum, and nothing follows it but sun protection.

  1. 01

    Morning application

    Apply to face and neck each morning, after the BioSignal Facial Serum. A pea-sized amount — enough to cover the whole face, and no more.

  2. 02

    Gentle upward massage

    Work it in with upward motions. This supports circulation and spreads the film evenly without dragging the skin downward.

  3. 03

    Allow it to set

    Wait two to three minutes before sun protection or make-up. The film needs that time to form, and the minerals to begin their work.

  4. 04

    Reapply if needed

    In very dry or harsh conditions, a light midday reapplication helps maintain the barrier and the hydration behind it.

06 Questions

Asked and answered.

Does it contain SPF?

No. Environmental Shield Day Cream does not contain SPF. It is designed as a protective layer that works alongside sun protection, not instead of it. Apply a broad-spectrum SPF once the cream has absorbed — two to three minutes after application.

Is it heavy or greasy?

No. It is formulated with a lightweight texture designed to absorb quickly and leave no residue, so it holds hydration through the day without the weight associated with traditional rich creams.

Can I wear make-up over it?

Yes. After two to three minutes for absorption it leaves a smooth, hydrated base. The lightweight texture is designed not to interfere with foundation or other cosmetic products.

How long does one bottle last?

With daily morning use of a pea-sized amount, one 50 ml bottle is designed to last approximately two to three months.

Do I need to reapply during the day?

In normal conditions the once-daily morning application is enough. In very dry, polluted or otherwise harsh environments, a light reapplication at midday can help maintain the protective film and hydration.

What makes the Pyrenean minerals different?

The mineral water is drawn from thermal springs at just over 1,800 metres in the Andorran Pyrenees. At altitude the water dissolves the rock it passes through differently, arriving with zinc, selenium, copper and magnesium held in ionic balance — the cofactors the skin’s own antioxidant enzymes require in order to function.

Can I use it without the serum?

You can, but the Longevity Protocol is designed as a sequence. The BioSignal Facial Serum carries the treatment; the day cream protects it and holds it in place. Used together they do what neither does alone.

Is it suitable for oily skin?

Yes. The lightweight, fast-absorbing texture is written for all skin types, including oily and combination skin. It supports hydration without adding excess oil or shine.
