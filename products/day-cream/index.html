01 The Formulation

A protective layer, not a rich cream.

Environmental Shield is written for the hours between leaving the house and coming home: particulate pollution, the oxidative load of bright light, and the quiet dehydration of air conditioning and central heating. It is a protective formulation that happens to moisturise, rather than a moisturiser with a claim attached.

Sweet almond oil and shea butter carry red algae and cucumber extracts over a Pyrenean mineral complex of zinc, selenium and copper. The texture is deliberately light: it settles into a breathable film across the surface instead of sitting on top of it, which is why it disappears under sun protection rather than arguing with it.

Beneath that film the minerals work at skin level. Zinc, selenium and copper are cofactors for antioxidant enzymes the skin already has — the enzymes exist regardless; the minerals are what let them run.

03 How To Use It

Four movements, every morning.

A protocol only works if it survives contact with an ordinary Tuesday. This is the shortest honest version — under two minutes, and the last thing you do before the day starts.

  1. Step one

    Warm

    A pea-sized amount, after the serum. Warm it between the fingertips until it turns fluid.

  2. Step two

    Press

    Press it into face and neck, outward and upward. Press — do not drag the skin downward.

  3. Step three

    Wait

    Two to three minutes. This is when it stops being a layer and becomes a base.

  4. Step four

    Protect

    Broad-spectrum sun protection over the set cream. Make-up after that, if you wear it.

In very dry, very cold or heavily polluted conditions, a light reapplication at midday is sensible. In an ordinary week, once is enough.

05 Questions

Asked and answered.

Does it contain SPF?

No. Environmental Shield Day Cream carries no SPF rating and is not a substitute for sunscreen. It is designed to work alongside sun protection, not instead of it. Apply a broad-spectrum SPF once the cream has set — two to three minutes after application.

What will I notice on the first morning?

Skin should feel comfortable and look more even the moment the cream is on, and within two to three minutes it settles to a smooth base that make-up sits well over. That is the immediate effect, and it is there from the first application. The environmental barrier is the other half — that one builds with daily use and is not something any single morning shows you.

Can I wear make-up over it?

Yes. Give it two to three minutes to set and it leaves a smooth, hydrated base. The texture is written to be light enough that foundation and sun protection spread over it rather than lifting it.

Is it heavy? Will it suit oily skin?

It is formulated as a lightweight cream that absorbs quickly and leaves no residue, and it is written for all skin types including oily and combination skin. It supports hydration without adding shine.

What makes the Pyrenean minerals different?

The mineral water is drawn from high-altitude springs high in the Andorran Pyrenees. At altitude, water dissolves the rock it passes through differently, arriving with zinc, selenium, copper and magnesium held in ionic balance — the cofactors the skin’s own antioxidant enzymes require in order to function.

Can I use it without the serum?

You can, but the Longevity Protocol is designed as a sequence. The BioSignal Facial Serum carries the treatment; the day cream protects it and holds it in place. Used together they do what neither does alone.
