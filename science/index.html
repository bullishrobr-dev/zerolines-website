02 The Mechanisms

Three actives.
One conversation.

Hydrolysed collagen, botanical signal peptides and a Pyrenean mineral complex. Three different materials addressing one failure — the loss of instruction between cells, rather than the loss of the cells themselves.

01 Hydrolysed Collagen

Fragments the skin reads as instruction.

Whole collagen is an enormous molecule — far too large to cross the barrier. This is the quiet problem with collagen creams: the protein sits on the surface, holds water there rather well, and never reaches the layer where structure is actually made.

Hydrolysed collagen is the same protein, cleaved by enzymes into peptide fragments a fraction of the original size. The fragments are the interesting part. In the body, free collagen peptides appear when the matrix has been damaged — so fibroblasts are tuned to read them as evidence that rebuilding is required.

That is what we formulate for: fragment size, purity and delivery, rather than the headline percentage on a label. A large quantity of a molecule that cannot arrive is not a strong formulation.

Designed to prompt the skin’s own synthesis — never to substitute for it.

02 Botanical Signal Peptides

Cellular communication, restored.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the vocabulary cells use to instruct one another. Ours are drawn from plants rather than synthesised: rose and chamomile flower waters, watermelon extract, and a botanical complex of witch hazel, calendula, chamomile, horse chestnut, peppermint, yarrow, mallow, linden and St John’s wort.

The intent is not to add collagen from the outside. It is to restore the instruction that tells skin to make its own — the message it sent freely at twenty-five and sends reluctantly at fifty.

Botanical rather than synthetic is a formulation decision, not a marketing one. Plant-derived complexes arrive as mixtures, and mixtures behave more like the layered, redundant signalling that skin evolved to read.

We are not the repair. We are the instructions.

03 Mineral Bioactives

Ionic balance from the Pyrenees.

Every formulation begins with water drawn from one high-altitude spring high in the Pyrenees. At altitude, water dissolves the rock it passes through differently — arriving carrying zinc, selenium, copper and magnesium in ionic balance, concentrated by geology over a span no laboratory can shortcut.

Those minerals are not decoration. They are cofactors: the elements the skin’s own antioxidant and repair enzymes require in order to work at all. Without them the instruction arrives and nothing can act on it.

03 Formulation Standards
Made in Barcelona

Engineered in Catalonia.

Every Zero Lines formulation is developed and manufactured in Barcelona. Catalonia has become one of Europe’s most serious regions for cosmetic science — advanced formulation sitting alongside a long culture of precision manufacturing.

The laboratory works to European cosmetic regulation. Every batch is subject to quality control and stability testing before release. This is not mass production; it is small-batch biotechnology, and the batch size is a constraint we accept rather than a story we tell.

We do not make claims we cannot substantiate. We describe what our compounds are designed to do, we document how they are made, and we let the biology speak at its own pace.
