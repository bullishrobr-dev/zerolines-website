# Five prompts for "the house"

The gap on the Story page is not a missing founder — it is that nobody appears
to make any of this. A house answers "who is behind it" with the room, the
hands, and the work. Aesop and Augustinus Bader do exactly this and neither
leads with a face.

Generate all five if you can. They are written as a set: same light, same
palette, same restraint, so they hang together on one page. If you only do
three, do **1, 2 and 4**.

**Settings for every one:** nano-banana-pro · 2K · quality high.
*(Not 4K — it failed twice at 89% earlier and charged both times.)*

**Paste the negative prompt into every single one.** Six images with competitor
branding and one with garbled text reached the live site before we started
checking, and every one was caught by eye rather than by search. The negative
prompt is the cheapest defence there is.

---

## The negative prompt — use on all five

```
text, letters, words, numbers, signage, labels, printed graduations, measurement
marks, etched text, garbled text, pseudo text, watermark, logo, brand name,
monogram, poster, book titles, lab coat, white coat, microscope, clipboard,
screens, monitors, clinical laboratory, hospital, medical equipment, syringes,
extra fingers, six fingers, missing fingers, deformed hands, malformed anatomy,
melted jewellery, bright cyan, electric blue, neon, teal and orange grade, HDR,
lens flare, starburst, heavy vignette, oversaturated, stock photo cheesiness,
plastic skin, heavy retouching
```

## 1 · The room, with someone in it — **16:9**

*For the top of a new "The house" section. The wide establishing shot.*

```
Interior photography of a small formulation studio in a converted textile
warehouse in Poblenou, Barcelona. Tall arched industrial windows down the left
wall, soft north light falling across a long pale travertine bench. Warm
limewashed ivory walls, original timber roof beams, a wide-plank pale oak floor.

One woman of about 45, South Asian or Mediterranean, stands at the far end of
the bench in three-quarter view, small in the frame — perhaps a fifth of its
height — wearing a simple ivory linen apron over a soft grey shirt. She is
looking down at her work, unaware of the camera. The room is the subject; she
gives it scale and purpose.

On the bench: a row of plain unmarked blown-glass vessels holding pale
translucent cream, a small brass funnel, a folded linen cloth in muted sea-teal.
Every glass surface is completely plain — no printed scales, no etched lines, no
labels of any kind. Nothing on the walls.

Palette: warm ivory, pale travertine, oak, clear glass, soft grey, with the one
small muted teal accent. The teal is desaturated, like oxidised copper, never a
bright cyan.

Shot on medium format, 35mm, f/5.6, camera at chest height and perfectly level —
architectural precision, verticals parallel. Natural window light only, no
artificial fill. True colour, no HDR, no colour grade. Quiet, expensive,
unhurried. The register of an Aesop or Augustinus Bader brand film still.
```

## 2 · Hands at the bench — **3:2**

*The most important one. Anonymous, human, and it carries the whole idea of care
without needing a face or a name.*

```
Extreme close photography of a pair of women's hands at work on a pale
travertine bench. Hands of someone about 45 — real skin, visible knuckles and
fine natural lines, short unpainted nails, no rings or jewellery of any kind.
One hand steadies a plain blown-glass vessel holding pale translucent cream; the
other tilts a small brass funnel above it. Both hands clearly and correctly
formed, five fingers each, natural articulation.

The glass is entirely plain — no printed graduations, no etched marks, no labels
anywhere in frame.

Framed close: hands, glass, funnel, a folded muted sea-teal linen cloth at the
edge. The face is not in frame at all. Warm ivory background falling softly out
of focus.

Light: one large soft north-facing window from the left, warm and even, a single
gentle shadow. Shot on medium format, 120mm macro, f/5.6, focus on the near hand
and the rim of the glass. Real skin texture kept, true colour, no HDR, no
retouching. Tactile, precise, unhurried.
```

## 3 · Two people, conferring — **16:9**

*Answers "who is behind this" as a plural. Reads as a house, not a person.*

```
Editorial photography of two women in a calm daylight formulation studio,
standing together at a pale stone bench, heads inclined toward a row of plain
glass vessels between them. One is about 45, Black, hair drawn back; the other
about 50, East Asian, greying hair in a short bob. Both wear simple ivory linen
aprons over soft grey and oatmeal shirts — no lab coats, no uniforms, no badges.

They are mid-conversation about the work, not posing: one gestures lightly
toward a vessel, the other is listening. Neither looks at the camera. Clear even
skin with almost no visible lines. Hands correctly formed with five fingers each.

The glassware is plain and unmarked throughout — no graduations, no labels, no
text on any surface anywhere in the frame.

The room: warm limewashed ivory walls, tall window out of frame to the left
casting soft north light, a shelf of plain clear vessels softly out of focus
behind them, one small brass funnel.

Palette: warm ivory, pale stone, clear glass, soft grey and oatmeal linen, skin,
with a single accent of muted desaturated sea-teal in a folded cloth. Never
bright cyan, no cold fluorescent cast.

Medium format, 65mm, f/4 — both women sharp, the shelf behind creamy. Real skin
texture, true colour, no HDR, no colour grade. The quiet of an Augustinus Bader
brand film.
```

## 4 · The one that can sit beside a signature — **4:5**

*If you ever want a "from the house" note with a portrait beside it, this is it.
Deliberately three-quarter and slightly turned, so it reads as one of the people
who make this rather than as a named founder.*

```
Editorial portrait of a woman of about 48, Mediterranean or Latina, standing in
three-quarter view and turned slightly away from the camera, looking off to the
side rather than into the lens. Composed, self-possessed, unhurried — the
expression of someone interrupted mid-thought, not someone posing. Clear even
skin with almost no visible lines, bare face, no makeup. Dark hair drawn back
simply, a few strands loose.

She wears a simple ivory linen apron over a soft grey shirt, sleeves pushed up.
No jewellery, no watch, no badge, no lettering on anything.

Background: a warm limewashed ivory wall in a daylight studio, softly out of
focus, with a sliver of tall window light at the edge. Generous empty space to
one side so a paragraph of text can sit beside her.

Palette: warm ivory, soft grey linen, bare skin, deep soft shadow. Entirely
natural colour — no teal cast on the skin, no colour filter, no monochrome
grade.

Light: one large soft north window from the front-left, white bounce from the
right, gentle modelling, a single soft shadow under the jaw. Medium format,
110mm, f/4 — tack sharp on the near eye, background creamy. Real skin texture
with visible pores kept. No plastic retouching, no beauty smoothing, no HDR.

Restrained, warm, quietly confident. A La Prairie or Augustinus Bader campaign
portrait, not a corporate headshot.
```

## 5 · The material, close — **3:2**

*A breathing space between the human images. Abstract, tactile, no anatomy risk.*

```
Extreme macro photography of pale translucent cream on a warm ivory ceramic
surface, drawn into a single soft ridge by a small brass spatula that has just
lifted away. The surface catches one long soft highlight along the crest of the
ridge; the texture is dense, matte and silken, not glossy.

Warm unbleached ivory throughout, luminous and pale, never dark. A single
whisper of muted sea-teal deep in the shadow of the fold — desaturated, low
chroma, barely there. No bright cyan.

No text, no packaging, no hands, no tools in frame except the edge of the brass
spatula leaving the shot.

Light: one large soft box high and slightly behind, raking across the ridge to
reveal its texture, gentle fill from the front. Medium format, 120mm macro,
f/11, focus stacked so the crest is tack sharp and the ground falls gently away.
True colour, no HDR, no glow, real photography of a real substance.

Tactile, restrained, expensive — a La Mer or Augustinus Bader texture campaign.
```

## Before you send them to me

Look at each one at full size and check, in this order:

1. **Any text at all?** On glass, on cloth, on a wall, in a reflection. Any word
   that is not "zero lines" is a reject — and garbled pseudo-text is a reject
   too. This is the failure that has cost us most.
2. **Count the fingers.** Every visible hand. This is the second most common.
3. **Faces:** women, apparent age 35–55, few or no lines, and across the set a
   genuine mixture. Anyone reading under 30 or over 60 is off-brief.
4. **Teal:** soft and desaturated, or bright and cyan? Bright is a reject.
5. **Does it look like a stock photo?** If yes, regenerate. That is the whole
   difference between this and everyone else.

If one fails, regenerate rather than send it — a near-miss on the Story page is
worse than an empty section.
