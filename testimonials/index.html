01 The Position

Why this page is empty.

The protocol is written. The collection is in pre-launch and not yet available to purchase. Nothing has been sold, and no one outside the laboratory has run a full cycle of the six-formulation protocol from beginning to end.

It would take an afternoon to invent eight names, attach them to eight stock portraits and call it evidence. A great deal of this industry does precisely that, and it is the reason almost nobody believes a testimonial page any more.

We are not willing to open our account with you by being untruthful about who has used the product. So the page stays as it is — honest, and briefly empty.

02 The Standard

What will appear here. And what never will.

Four rules, settled now — before there is any commercial pressure to bend them. They will not change once the accounts start arriving.

  1. 01

    Named

    A real person, named. First name and initial where privacy is asked for — never an invented one, never a stock portrait standing in for a customer.

  2. 02

    Unpaid

    Nothing is exchanged for an account of the protocol. No fee, no gifted product in return, no affiliate arrangement, no discount for a kind word.

  3. 03

    Unedited

    Published as written. We may trim for length, and we will mark where we have cut. We will not rewrite anybody’s verdict into something warmer.

  4. 04

    Dated

    With the length of use attached. A view at three weeks is a different thing from a view at six months, and you deserve to know which one you are reading.

One further rule, and it is the strictest. Anything said here about skin that we cannot substantiate does not go on the page: no percentages, no panel arithmetic, no clinical vocabulary we have not earned. An account is one person’s experience — we will present it as exactly that, and nothing more.
