The Longevity Protocol

Six formulations. One sequence.

Zero Lines is not a collection of products. It is an order — each formulation performing one biological function and preparing the skin for the one that follows. That is what separates a routine from a protocol.

Begin at step one How to start

“A routine is a habit. A protocol is a sequence — and the sequence is doing as much work as the formulations inside it.”

01 The Logic of Order

Why protocol sequencing matters.

How much of an ingredient reaches living tissue is decided by when it is applied, not only by what it contains. A serum laid over unprepared skin travels a fraction of the distance it would over a surface that has been cleared. A rich repair cream applied first becomes a lid on everything that should have gone beneath it.

So the protocol is written as an order. Clear the surface. Deliver the activation while the skin is most receptive. Keep the instruction current every day. Seal it in the morning against the environment and at night against the hours of repair.

The order is not arbitrary. It is the formulation.

The science beneath it →
Principle I

Prepare

Exfoliation leads. An active applied to a surface of accumulated dead cells meets debris before it meets living tissue.

Principle II

Deliver

Thinnest to richest, always. The water-based formulations travel; the creams that follow hold them in place.

Principle III

Repeat

The weekly steps set the instruction. The daily steps keep it current. Neither half of the protocol works alone.

02 The Six Steps

Reset, activate, signal.

The first three steps do the structural work. Two of them happen once a week, in this order and no other. The third runs every day of the year.

It is a recalibration of the canvas, so that what comes next lands on living skin rather than on the residue of the week behind it.

When
Once weekly, on cleansed skin, in the evening
Order
First. Before every other step in the protocol
Key
Aloe vera juice · brown algae extract

03 The Weekly Rhythm

Two minutes, twice a day.

Written out, the protocol is short. Two steps in the morning, two at night, and one evening a week where the sequence opens with renewal and activation before the daily steps resume.

Every morning
  1. 03 Signal
    BioSignal Facial Serum, on clean skin
  2. 04 Shield
    Environmental Shield Day Cream, then sun protection
Every night
  1. 03 Signal
    The same serum. The instruction does not pause
  2. 05 Restore
    Renewal and Repair Night Cream, last of all
One evening a week

01 Renew, then 02 Activate — the peeling gel first, the syringe within minutes of it, then the evening’s serum and night cream as usual.

Once a year

06 Sustain — a fresh cartridge in the same instrument, and the protocol simply continues without a break in the signal.

04 Beginning the Protocol

Begin gradually. The barrier sets the pace.

Introducing six formulations on the same evening asks a great deal of a barrier that has met none of them before. We recommend building the protocol in stages, over roughly ninety days.

  1. 01

    Weeks one and two

    The serum morning and night; the day cream each morning. Nothing else. Hydration and protection first — this is the base the rest of the protocol is laid on.

  2. 02

    Weeks three and four

    Add the night cream. The evening becomes serum then cream, and the skin begins to do its repair work with something richer to hold it in place.

  3. 03

    Weeks five and six

    Add the collagen syringe, once weekly, on a single evening. The first of the two weekly steps, and the first structural instruction of the protocol.

  4. 04

    Week seven onwards

    Add the peeling gel on that same weekly evening, immediately before the syringe. The full sequence is now running in its intended order.

After twelve weeks, the protocol is simply the protocol: two weekly steps, four daily ones, and a refill when the cartridge runs out. Every skin arrives at that point differently — our analyser will adjust the sequence to your climate, your concerns and the routine you already have.
