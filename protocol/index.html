01 The Two Effects

One routine, two timescales.

The shape of every Zero Lines formulation, and the reason the range is a protocol rather than a shelf. Read the six steps below twice: down the left for tonight, down the right for the year.

Effect one

Immediately.

Visible from the very first application, that same day. On the Line Corrector it is the clearest: placed along an expression line, the skin looks visibly smoother within minutes and holds that look through the day.

Like makeup, except it is skincare.

This half is temporary, and it repeats every time you apply. It is also the whole reason the Refill exists — some people will only ever want this effect, and they should be able to keep having it.

Effect two

Over time.

Underneath that, with consistent use across weeks, the formulations are designed to support the skin’s own structure — so that lines have less to return to when expression relaxes.

Slower to show. Structural when it does.

Every formulation in the protocol is built around this pairing — the visible change you notice, and the slower work underneath it. How quickly each shows will differ from person to person.

02 Why the Order

The order is for the second effect.

The immediate effect does not need a protocol. Any one of the six, used alone and out of sequence, will still give you its same-day result — that half works at the surface, and the surface is right there.

The lasting effect is the one that depends on order — on how you layer each formulation, and how much of it ever reaches living tissue. A serum laid over an unprepared surface travels a fraction of the distance. A rich cream applied first becomes a lid on everything meant to go beneath it.

Applied out of order, six good products. Applied in order, a system.

05 What to Expect

Honestly: what happens, and when.

One half of this you can judge on day one. The other asks for patience — and we would rather say so here than let you measure it against the wrong calendar.

The first week

The immediate effect is there from the first application — the Line Corrector on the evening you first place it, the peeling gel the moment you rinse. Nothing structural is visible yet. This week is for the routine to settle and for skin to meet formulations it has not met before. If anything feels reactive, slow the introduction down.

The first month

The same-day effect becomes familiar and repeatable, which is the point of it. Alongside it, the everyday things: skin comfortable through the afternoon, make-up sitting more evenly. The lasting effect is under way and still invisible — anyone promising otherwise at four weeks is selling you the first effect twice.

Eight to twelve weeks

Skin renews on its own schedule, and the deeper layers move slower than the surface. This is the earliest honest window for the second effect to read as more than a good day: the look more even overall, expression lines softer at rest rather than only after application, and each application starting from a better baseline.

What we will not tell you

No percentage, no panel, no trial, no before-and-after. Zero Lines has not launched and has nothing to cite, so nothing is cited. Every statement on this page describes how skin looks and what the formulations are designed to support — never a permanent change to what skin is.

Consistency does more here than intensity, and the first thirty days are mostly about settling into the routine. The protocol is written to be survivable on a bad week: four small applications a day, one longer evening, one cartridge a year.

If your skin is reactive, or you are pregnant or under a dermatologist’s care, take this page to them before you begin. External use only. This is not a medical treatment.
