Commitment

Built to be used. By everyone.

Zero Lines is committed to digital accessibility. We keep improving the experience for everyone, and we apply the relevant standards rather than claiming a badge and moving on.

Target · WCAG 2.1 AA Last reviewed · May 2026
01 Our Standards

Level AA, of WCAG 2.1.

We aim to conform to level AA of the World Wide Web Consortium’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1. Those guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities — and, in practice, more usable for everybody else.

Conformance is not a certificate you hang on a wall. It is a moving target that every new page can break, which is why this page carries an email address rather than a logo.

AA

02 What We Do

Seven habits, kept every build.

  • Semantic HTML structure for screen reader compatibility 01
  • Keyboard navigation support throughout the site 02
  • Sufficient colour contrast ratios for readability 03
  • Text alternatives for all meaningful images 04
  • Visible focus indicators on every interactive element 05
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences, including every animation on this site 06
  • Clear, consistent navigation and labelling 07
01

Third-party content

Our website may link to third-party sites or embed third-party content. While we cannot control the accessibility of external platforms, we choose partners who share our commitment to inclusivity.

02

Assessment

We review the site regularly using both automated tooling and manual testing — keyboard-only passes, screen reader passes, and a check that every animated element still reads with motion disabled. This statement was last reviewed in May 2026. We are committed to ongoing improvement and we welcome your input.

Feedback & Support

Tell us what we got wrong.

If anything on this site is difficult to reach, read or operate, we would genuinely like to know — with the page, the device and the assistive technology you were using, if you can. We take the report seriously and make every reasonable effort to fix it.
