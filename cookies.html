Cookie policy.

What cookies are, how Zero Lines uses them, and how you change your mind at any point without leaving this page.

Last updated · May 2026 Questions · privacy@zerolines.life
What this site sets today

Zero Lines is a pre-launch site with no shop and no advertising pipeline. The only thing we store on your device right now is the choice you make in the notice at the bottom of the screen — a single key, zl_cookie_choice, held in your browser’s local storage. If you decline the non-essential categories, nothing else is written.

01 Four Categories

Not all cookies do the same work.

Only the first category is required for the site to function. The other three are yours to permit or refuse, and refusing them costs you nothing but a slightly less tailored experience.

  1. 01

    Essential

    Required for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.

  2. 02

    Performance

    Help us understand how visitors interact with the site by collecting anonymous data. Today we use none of these: our traffic measurement is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie, follows nobody between sites, and builds no profile. It therefore runs whether or not you accept anything below, because there is nothing to consent to.

  3. 03

    Functionality

    Remember your preferences, such as language selection and saved choices.

  4. 04

    Targeting

    Used to deliver relevant advertising and measure it. Only ever with your consent.

02 The Policy
01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how you interact with content, and improve your overall experience.

02

Managing your preferences

You can change your choice at any time using the control further down this page, or through your browser settings — most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies entirely. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of this website behave.

03

Third-party cookies

We may use trusted third-party services — an analytics provider, for example — that set their own cookies. Those providers have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to read. We do not sell data to anyone, and we do not permit partners to use it for their own advertising.

04

Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation or our own practices. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised date.

05

Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, write to privacy@zerolines.life. Our wider data practices are set out in the privacy policy.

Your Choice

Change your mind, freely.

Consent should be as easy to withdraw as it was to give. This clears the choice stored in your browser and brings the notice back, so you can answer it again.
