01 The Science

How Zero Lines actually works.

The Longevity Protocol isn’t marketing language. It is a structured approach to skin cell signalling that has been studied, tested and refined over four years of development.

What is the Longevity Protocol, in plain terms?

The Longevity Protocol is a method of using six formulations in a specific order, so that each step prepares the skin for the next.

Traditional skincare often piles active ingredients together without considering how they interact. The protocol sequences them across six steps — Renew, Activate, Signal, Shield, Restore, Sustain — beginning with gentle weekly renewal, then targeted activation with the Line Corrector, then daily signalling with the serum, then protection by day, repair by night, and the refill that keeps the first effect available whenever you want it. Each step creates the conditions for the next.

No single product carries the result on its own. The steps compound — each one prepares the next, so the sequence delivers more than the same six formulations would apart.

Is there clinical evidence behind the formulations?

Every active ingredient in the Zero Lines range has been selected on the basis of peer-reviewed research. We do not use ingredients because they are trendy — we use them because independent literature describes measurable effects on skin structure and function.

Our signal peptide complex is built on research into collagen and elastin production over eight to twelve weeks of consistent application. Our botanical peptide blend draws on studies of mechanical surface tension reduction. The ceramide complex in our barrier formulations rests on extensive dermatological literature about transepidermal water loss.

That is evidence for the ingredients, not a completed trial of a finished product — the collection has not launched, so no such trial exists yet, and we will not pretend otherwise. We publish our ingredient rationale and reference the key studies on the science page.

What is the difference between the immediate effect and the lasting effect?

The immediate effect is what you see from the very first application: with the Line Corrector, expression lines look visibly smoother within minutes and hold that look through the day. It is a change in the way the skin’s own surface sits and reflects light, from the botanical peptide blend and the hydration architecture around it.

The lasting effect is what consistent use is designed to build underneath, over weeks and months — the skin’s own structure, its barrier and its elasticity. This is the work of the signal peptide complex and sustained nutrient delivery.

Most skincare offers one or the other. Every Zero Lines formulation is built to do both at once — something you can see today, and something that keeps building underneath.

How long until I see results?

The immediate effect is designed to be visible from the very first application, the same day: smoother-looking texture, a more hydrated appearance, a subtle luminosity. These are changes in the way the skin’s own surface sits — not an optical illusion produced by silicones or fillers.

The lasting effect builds progressively. The protocol is designed for firmness and elasticity to become noticeable across eight to twelve weeks — the earliest honest window, and the same one the protocol page gives, with the structural qualities it targets — collagen density, barrier function — accumulating across eight to twelve weeks of consistent use.

Skin is living tissue. It responds to signals over time. The protocol is written for someone who wants both: the change they can see today, and the one that goes on underneath. Individual results vary.

Why do you focus on skin structure rather than just wrinkles?

Because wrinkles are a symptom, not the cause — and a fine line is not the same thing as a wrinkle. The visible lines and slackness that concern most people are the result of deeper structural changes: collagen depletion, elastin fragmentation, barrier dysfunction and slowed cellular turnover.

If you only address the surface — filling lines temporarily, masking texture with silicones — you are managing symptoms while the underlying structure continues to degrade. Zero Lines addresses the architecture: supporting the matrix that gives skin its shape, resilience and capacity for self-repair.

This is why the protocol is not built around a temporary optical effect. It is built for structural support that lasts because the skin itself is in better condition.

02 The Protocol

Using Zero Lines correctly.

The Longevity Protocol is a ritual, not a routine. Order, timing and consistency all contribute to the final result. Here is how to get the most from the formulations.

What is the exact order of the protocol?

Morning, daily:

1. Cleanse with your regular cleanser
2. Apply BioSignal Facial Serum to damp skin
3. Wait 60–90 seconds for absorption
4. Apply Environmental Shield Day Cream

Evening, daily:

1. Cleanse thoroughly
2. Apply BioSignal Facial Serum
3. Wait 60–90 seconds
4. Apply Renewal and Repair Night Cream

Weekly, once or twice:

After evening cleansing, apply Bio-Renewal Peeling Gel to dry skin. Leave for up to two minutes — no longer — then rinse and continue with serum and night cream. Do not use the peeling gel on consecutive days.

Weekly, targeted:

On the same evening, minutes after the peeling gel has been rinsed, apply the Precision Collagen Renewal Line Corrector to the areas you want to work on, before the serum. The twelve-month refill continues that step once the first cartridge is finished.

Do I need to use all six formulations?

The six formulations are designed to work as an integrated system. Each one creates the conditions for the next to perform optimally. Using the full protocol will deliver the most complete and lasting result.

That said, we understand that some people prefer to start gradually. The serum is the structural cornerstone of the protocol — if you begin with one formulation, begin there. The day and night creams can be introduced next, the peeling gel added once your skin has adapted to the daily rhythm, and the Precision Collagen Renewal Line Corrector layered in as the weekly, targeted step.

We do not recommend using the peeling gel without the serum and a cream, as the skin needs the subsequent nourishment and protection after exfoliation.

How much product should I use?

Serum: 2–3 drops for the face, one additional drop for the neck and décolletage. The serum spreads easily on damp skin. More is not better — the active concentration is calibrated for this amount.

Day Cream: a pea-sized amount for the face. Warm it between your fingertips before pressing it gently into the skin. Add a second pea for the neck if you wish.

Night Cream: a little more than the day cream — roughly two pea-sized portions. The night formulation is richer and designed to sustain the skin through eight hours of repair activity.

Peeling Gel: a thin, even layer across the face, avoiding the eye area. You do not need a thick coating — the enzymes work at the surface and do not require occlusion.

Line Corrector: a single measured dose from the cartridge, worked only into the areas you are treating. It is a targeted step, not a mask.

Can I use other products alongside Zero Lines?

Yes, with consideration. The Longevity Protocol is a complete system, but it can coexist with other products if you understand the sequencing.

Compatible: gentle cleansers, sunscreen (always applied after the day cream), eye creams and hydrating mists. These do not interfere with the active ingredients.

Use with caution: retinoids, high-concentration vitamin C and other strong actives. These can be used, but not in the same application as the Zero Lines serum. If you use a retinoid, apply it on alternate nights, or after the night cream has fully absorbed — thirty minutes or more.

Avoid combining: other exfoliating acids or peels on the same day as the Bio-Renewal Peeling Gel. That risks over-exfoliation and a compromised barrier.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing dramatic. Skin biology operates on timescales of weeks, not hours. Missing one day will not reset your progress.

Consistency does matter, though. The signalling peptides in the serum work best when they meet the skin regularly — that maintains the conversation between the formula and your cells. Think of it like exercise: one missed session is irrelevant, but an irregular pattern slows cumulative progress.

If you miss several days, simply resume the protocol. There is no need to make up for lost time by using extra product. The formulations are calibrated for daily use, and exceeding the recommended amount does not accelerate anything.

03 Ingredients & Safety

What goes into the formula.

Transparency is non-negotiable. Every ingredient is chosen for a specific biological purpose, and we tell you exactly what it does and why it is there.

Are Zero Lines products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. The formulations are designed with sensitivity in mind. We avoid the common irritants: no artificial fragrances, no drying alcohols, no sulphates, no parabens, and no silicones that mask skin issues rather than address them.

That said, “sensitive skin” is not a single condition. If you have a history of reacting to active ingredients, we recommend patch testing: apply a small amount of serum behind your ear or on your inner forearm for 24 hours before using it on the face.

The Peeling Gel contains natural enzymes that are gentler than acid exfoliants, but if your skin is very reactive, start with once weekly rather than twice, and watch how it responds.

Are the products safe during pregnancy?

Zero Lines formulations do not contain retinoids, hydroquinone, salicylic acid, or other ingredients typically contraindicated during pregnancy. Our actives are peptides, ceramides, botanical extracts and hydration factors — all generally recognised as safe.

Pregnancy changes skin sensitivity and reactivity in unpredictable ways, however. We strongly recommend consulting your obstetrician or dermatologist before introducing any new skincare during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Take the named actives with you: each formulation page lists what is in it and what each one does. Full INCI lists are published on the packaging, and on this site, when the formulations go on sale.

Where does the water in your formulas come from?

We use Pyrenean mineral water, drawn from a protected valley in the Andorran Pyrenees, at just over 1,800 metres. That water carries a mineral profile which supports skin hydration at a cellular level.

Most skincare uses purified tap water or distilled water, which is chemically inert and offers no biological benefit. Pyrenean water carries trace minerals — zinc, selenium, copper, magnesium — that act as cofactors for the skin’s own antioxidant enzymes. It is one of the details that separates a functional formula from an exceptional one.

Do you test on animals?

No. Zero Lines does not test on animals, and we do not work with suppliers who do. Safety and tolerance assessment is carried out on human volunteers under dermatological supervision.

That assessment involves patch testing, instrumental measurement — corneometry, cutometry and VISIA imaging — and self-assessment questionnaires. It gives us far more relevant data than animal testing ever could: human skin is the only skin our products will ever touch.

Are your products vegan?

The majority of our ingredients are plant-derived or synthetically produced. Our peptide complexes, however, are bio-engineered using fermentation processes that may involve trace dairy-derived substrates in the growth medium.

We are actively working toward a fully vegan formulation and expect to reach that certification within the next production cycle. If vegan status is essential for you, please contact us and we will write to you the moment the certification is complete.

05 About Zero Lines

The story and the philosophy.

Zero Lines was founded on a single conviction: that the skincare industry has been asking the wrong questions for decades. Here is what we believe — and what we refuse to compromise.

Why “Zero Lines”? What does the name mean?

The name is intentionally provocative — and intentionally misunderstood. Most people assume “Zero Lines” refers to zero wrinkles. It does not.

“Zero Lines” refers to the line between skincare and medicine, between beauty and biology, between what the industry sells and what skin actually needs. We erase that line. We do not believe in cosmetic approaches that ignore biology, or medical approaches that ignore the experience of using a product.

The zero is also a circle — a symbol of wholeness, of systems thinking, of the closed loop between formulation science and skin response. We do not treat skin as a surface to be decorated. We treat it as a system to be supported.

Where are Zero Lines products made?

All Zero Lines formulations are developed, formulated and manufactured in Barcelona, Spain. Our laboratory operates under EU cosmetic regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, among the strictest regulatory frameworks in the world.

The company is headquartered in Gibraltar, with the research and production facility in Barcelona. The dual location reflects our identity: Gibraltar as a point of strategic international connection, Barcelona as a city with deep traditions in both pharmaceutical science and design culture.

Who is behind Zero Lines?

Zero Lines was founded by a team of biochemists, dermatological researchers and formulation specialists who spent years inside the skincare industry before deciding it needed to change. We have worked for major cosmetic houses, pharmaceutical companies and independent laboratories.

What unites us is a shared frustration: the gap between what science knows and what the industry sells. We started Zero Lines to close that gap — to make products as honest as they are effective, and as beautiful as they are biologically sound.

How can I get in touch?

We read every message. For questions, press enquiries, partnerships, or simply to tell us how the protocol is going, write to info@zerolines.life or use the contact page.

Our Barcelona laboratory welcomes visits from press, prospective retail partners and researchers. If you would like to arrange one, mention it in your email and we will coordinate.

Response time is typically within 24 hours on business days. We are a small team, but we are committed to genuine human contact — not automated replies.
